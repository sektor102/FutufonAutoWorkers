Description
Futufon AutoWorker does the factory packaging work for you: press F8 at the workbench and Full Cycle mode automatically collects/refills supplies, assembles complete charger packages, fills a shipping carton and delivers it to a pallet. It uses the game's own items and assembly logic; normal production targets, attendance and pay rules still apply.

Installation instructions
1. Close the game and copy FutufonAutoWorkerMscModLoader.dll from the archive into your active MSCLoader Mods folder.
2. Load your save and choose the mode, batch size and speed under MSCLoader > Futufon AutoWorker.
3. Clock in at Futufon, stand at a clear workbench, look down at the tabletop and press F8. Leave space beside the table for the shipping carton and a free slot on a nearby pallet.
Stay by the workbench during assembly. The mod stops at the selected batch limit; walking away requests a pause.
F8: start / soft pause / resume (finishes the current small package). F6: toggle HUD. Assign the mode-switch key in MSCLoader keybindings.

Main features
- Full Cycle: automatic supplies, assembly, packing and pallet delivery.
- Manual Delivery: the mod fills the carton; you carry it to the pallet.
- Loose Packages: automatic supplies and assembly; you pack and deliver the finished packages.
- Manual Supplies: you bring/open the materials; the mod assembles packages; you pack and deliver them.
- One carton holds 44 small packages. Fill Pallet produces up to four cartons/176 packages, depending on free slots.
- Adjustable speed and soft pause/resume.
- Russian/English HUD: progress, stock, shift times, overtime balance and reminders.
Public test release; feedback and bug reports are welcome.
