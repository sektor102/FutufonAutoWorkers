1.2.0 — 8 October 2026

- Diagnostic shortcuts are unassigned by default. Old F7/F9 defaults are not imported.
- Assignable next-mode hotkey; mode changes apply between complete packages and keep progress, cartons and stacks.
- Missing lower filled layers in shipping cartons are displayed, including after save loading. Only visual meshes are added; native counters are retained.
- HUD displays current/last shift clock-in and clock-out times and the native signed overtime balance.
- Tuesday-only HUD reminder for the 13:10 meeting.
- HUD key hints reflect assigned bindings.

Public test release. Build and automated checks passed for all 16 mode transitions, mixed outputs, partial-carton continuation, pause/resume, 44/176 limits, shift reconstruction, overtime display and lower-row counts. Physical actions are simulated in scheduler tests. Wider in-game testing of new transitions, visuals and HUD is still needed.
